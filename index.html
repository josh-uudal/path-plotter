Measurements

          

          

          

            
            
          

          
Measurements are editor furniture: they save with the project,
            never reach the Java, and never appear in the window preview or an exported
            PNG. They keep the coordinates they were taken at, so moving a shape afterwards
            leaves its measurement where it was.
